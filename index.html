Accuracy includes only locked UP/DOWN predictions with exact TWAP60 values at both boundaries. WAIT calls are excluded from accuracy and included in coverage denominator. Stored on this device.
Polymarket odds are derived from the live CLOB (Central Limit Order Book): the live buy and sell offers for UP/DOWN shares. Best bid/ask midpoints are normalized across UP/DOWN; these are market-implied prices, not guaranteed win probabilities. Binance BTC/USDT is only the exchange chart feed. Chainlink BTC/USD TWAP60 is the official target reference. The public order book is commonly called the CLOB (Central Limit Order Book): bids are buy offers and asks are sell offers. VVS direction is calculated exclusively from Polymarket BTC 5m CLOB odds, their short-term movement, order-book depth imbalance and recent Polymarket UP/DOWN trade flow. No Binance candles, exchange prices, RSI, EMA or external BTC momentum enters the pick. No trades are placed.
