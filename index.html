    What VVS predicts: which side, UP or DOWN, resolves as the winner when the entire 5-minute round ends, not which way BTC or the odds are moving right now. About 60 seconds in, it reads the market-implied chance each side wins (from live Polymarket prices), checks how the odds behaved across the whole round (average level, reversals), and uses order-book depth and trades only as confirmation that matters less as time runs out. It then picks the side with the stronger evidence and locks it. It never skips, and no exchange price or momentum indicator is used. Wins and losses count only the locked call against Polymarket's resolved result.
    Limits: this follows the market favorite. The market price already reflects what traders know, so the win rate should land near the average odds at entry, which is not a profit edge, and buying costs the ask and fees. Backtests use trade prices, not historical order books, and cannot replay depth. History is stored in this browser only. VVS can't run while Safari is closed.
