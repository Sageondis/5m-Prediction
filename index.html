Polymarket odds are derived from the live CLOB (Central Limit Order Book): the live buy and sell offers for UP/DOWN shares. Best bid/ask midpoints are normalized across UP/DOWN; these are market-implied prices, not guaranteed win probabilities. Binance BTC/USDT is only the exchange chart feed. Chainlink BTC/USD TWAP60 is the official target reference. The public order book is commonly called the CLOB (Central Limit Order Book): bids are buy offers and asks are sell offers. VVS selects UP or DOWN only when Polymarket strongly favors one side and chart direction plus momentum confirm it; otherwise it records SKIP for the entire round. Chart estimates are heuristic and uncalibrated. No trades are placed.
