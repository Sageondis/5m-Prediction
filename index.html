    How the call is made: about 60 seconds into each round, VVS combines live Polymarket odds (primary), odds movement from Polymarket's price history, order-book depth and recent trades into one score, picks the side it favors, then locks it for the round. It never skips when live data is available. No exchange price or technical indicator is used. Wins and losses count only the locked call against Polymarket's resolved result, and unresolved rounds are not counted.
    Limits: this follows the market favorite. The market price already reflects what traders know, so the win rate should land near the average odds at entry, which is not a profit edge, and buying costs the ask and fees. Backtests can replay only odds and movement, not depth or trades. History is stored in this browser only. VVS can't run while Safari is closed.
